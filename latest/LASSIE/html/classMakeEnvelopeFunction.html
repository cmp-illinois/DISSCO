Detailed Description

MakeEnvelope: a dynamic list of (X, Y, interpolation, proportion) points plus a Scale factor.

<Fun><Name>MakeEnvelope</Name>
<Xs><X>v</X>...</Xs>
<Ys><Y>v</Y>...</Ys>
<Types><T>LINEAR|SPLINE|EXPONENTIAL</T>...</Types>
<Pros><P>FLEXIBLE|FIXED</P>...</Pros>
<Scale>v</Scale>
</Fun>
